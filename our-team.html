About Shiloh

Our Team

Landon Lyle, Founder

Landon started on his uncle Mark's crew at 16, hauling cedar shake debris to the dumpster. It taught him the job before the trade: work hard, leave the site clean, and know that every detail counts. A stray nail in the yard matters. So does how you carry a bundle up a ladder.

Landon went on to a career in healthcare and rose to Chief Operating Officer, leading operations, finance, sales, and client care for a large healthcare organization.

He founded Shiloh to bring that discipline home and carry the family trade into its fourth generation. When you call Shiloh, you work with Landon from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.
