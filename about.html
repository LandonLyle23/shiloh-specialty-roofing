About

Built the way we'd build our own home.

Shiloh Specialty Roofing LLC exists because most homes don't get roofed by people who actually work in the materials they're installing. We do.

Why We Exist

Specialty roofing, not volume roofing.

North Georgia has plenty of roofing companies built to run crews fast and move to the next job. That model works fine for a standard asphalt reroof. It falls apart on cedar, slate, standing seam metal, copper, and tile, materials where the difference between a 15-year roof and a 50-year roof is entirely in details a fast-moving crew doesn't have time to get right.

Shiloh was built around the opposite model: fewer shortcuts, more trade knowledge, and a crew that treats flashing, fastening, and ventilation like the structural details they actually are. [PLACEHOLDER: founding story and years in business. Supply specifics and we'll write this in properly].
