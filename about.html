About

Built the way we'd build our own home.

Shiloh Specialty Roofing LLC exists because most homes don't get roofed by people who actually work in the materials they're installing. We do.

Why We Exist

Most roofers can't install these materials. We can.

Cedar, slate, standing seam metal, copper, and tile are not asphalt with a different surface. They fail in specific ways when they're installed by crews who work in them once a year, and the failures don't show up for a decade.

North Georgia has plenty of roofing companies built to run crews fast and move to the next job. That model works fine for a standard asphalt reroof. It falls apart on specialty materials, where the difference between a 15 year roof and a 50 year roof is entirely in details a fast-moving crew doesn't have time to get right.

Thirty years in these materials is the whole difference, and it's the only thing we do.

How We Work

What that looks like on your project

We document everything

Every inspection is photographed and written up in detail, for our own estimate and for your insurance carrier if it's a storm claim.

We install to spec

Manufacturer nailing patterns, correct exposure, real flashing details. Not a faster pattern that technically holds but underperforms or voids the warranty.

Insured on every job

Every project is fully insured before a single shingle comes off. Ask any contractor for proof of coverage. If they hesitate, that's your answer.
