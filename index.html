Who We Are

A family roofing business, not a storm-chasing volume crew.

Shiloh Specialty Roofing is family owned and family run. Our family has been roofing in North Georgia for more than thirty years, and that is not a line on a website. It is why we know what cedar does in this humidity, why we flash a chimney the slow way, and why we will not put something on your house we would not put on our own.

The roof on a home should be installed with the same precision as the rest of the house. Cedar shake with correct exposure and weather-checking gaps. Standing seam metal with real hemmed seams instead of exposed screws. Slate and tile set the way the material wants to be set, not the way that is fastest.
