Who We Are

A specialty roofing contractor, not a storm-chasing volume crew.

Shiloh Specialty Roofing LLC is built around one idea: the roof on an expensive home should be installed with the same precision as the rest of the house. That means cedar shake with correct exposure and weather-checking gaps, standing seam metal with real hemmed seams instead of exposed screws, slate and tile set the way the material actually wants to be set — not the way that's fastest.

We work across north Georgia's high-end residential market, from new construction specialty roofing to storm and insurance restoration on existing homes.
