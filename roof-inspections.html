Roof Inspections

Wind and hail damage

Wind damage usually looks like creased, torn, or missing shingles, or tabs that lifted and broke their seal. Hail damage is subtler: bruising or fractures in the shingle mat, sometimes with granule loss that exposes the mat underneath. Soft metal, like vents, gutter aprons, and flashing, often shows hail hits more clearly than shingles do, and is usually the easier tell from the ground.
